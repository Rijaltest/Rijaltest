How to use

A simple routine, four easy steps.

  1. 1

    Start clean

    Wash and towel-dry your hair and beard so the oil can absorb properly.

  2. 2

    Apply

    Take a few drops of oil on your palm and rub your hands together to warm it up.

  3. 3

    Massage

    Work it in gently with your fingertips, right down to the skin.

  4. 4

    Comb through

    Finish with the Rijal comb for an even spread and a neat look.

Patch test before first use. Stop use if irritation occurs.
